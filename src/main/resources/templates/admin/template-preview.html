WHAT THE RENDERER OWNS

Fixed layout sections

The blocks below are always present; the layout controls how they look, and the fields above control their text. Company details and the logo come from each account's company profile, so a template never stores customer data.

  • Seller blockCompany name, address, GSTIN and logo placeholder, taken from the account profile at print time.
  • Customer blockBilled-to name, contact, address, GSTIN from the invoice's customer snapshot.
  • Invoice headerInvoice number, issue date, due date and payment state.
  • Line itemsDescription, quantity, unit price, discount and tax per line, ordered as entered.
  • TotalsSubtotal, discount, tax and grand total, computed server-side and snapshotted on the invoice.
  • Payment terms & notesEditable text blocks from this template.
  • FooterEditable text block from this template.
